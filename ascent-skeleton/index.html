Some FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you accept?

We welcome children from 18 months up to 6 years old in our tailored programs.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

Our average ratio is 1 teacher for every 6 to 8 students to ensure individualized care.

How can parents monitor child progress?

We provide weekly digital updates, photos, and monthly one-on-one progress meetings.
